# Agent runbook

## Work your queue

Open the oldest ticket in your team's queue. Read the whole thread before you reply.

## Approve a refund

Billing agents handle refund requests. For each one:

1. Check the order and the payment in the billing console.
2. Decide whether the refund is due under the refund policy.
3. If it is, click **Approve refund**. The payment provider sends the money at once, and it cannot be pulled back.
4. Reply to the customer with the amount and the date.

About 30 refund requests arrive each day.
